# Sea freight quote (ZIM) — MINYALA, Egypt → France

Checked 13/10/2026. ZIM's website blocks automated access, so its quote form could not be read: the field list below is what ZIM's booking pages and carriers generally ask for. Fill the form yourself at [zim.com → Request a Quote](https://www.zim.com/tools/request-a-quote) or ask a freight forwarder.

## What to enter

| Field | What to put | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Company | Business name as registered, SIREN 985 289 065, address, contact, VAT number | ZIM's portal asks for a VAT number ([myZIM FAQ](https://www.zim.com/help/personal-area-faq)). A quote is valid only for the customer named on it ([ZIM quotation terms](https://www.zim.com/fr/help/quotation-terms-conditions)), so the business that will book must request it. |
| Origin | **Damietta, Egypt** (port of loading) + the supplier's factory or warehouse if ZIM should collect it | Damietta is the only Egyptian port confirmed on a direct ZIM loop to Le Havre in Oct 2026: the weekly ZNI "Israel Express" (Hamburg–Antwerp–Le Havre–Ashdod–Haifa–Damietta–Valencia–London–Rotterdam–Hamburg) ([Port of Hamburg](https://www.hafen-hamburg.de/en/services/israel-express-zni/)). Ask ZIM to confirm the route and transit time; Le Havre is near the end of the loop. |
| Destination | **Le Havre**, France. Final place: Gennevilliers (HAROPA container terminal) if ZIM or a haulier carries on | Gennevilliers has a container terminal linked to Le Havre by river, rail and road ([HAROPA 2026](https://www.haropaport.com/sites/default/files/media/downloads/catalogue-terminaux-conteneurs-fr-2026.pdf)). Vernon has no container terminal; the nearest is Limay-Porcheville. |
| Container | **1 × 20' dry (20DV)**, ask for a box rated **30,480 kg max gross** | Stone fills a container by weight, not volume; a 40' carries no more. The rating is on each box's CSC plate and not every 20' is rated this high, so ask ZIM to confirm. |
| Cargo weight | Gross weight per container from the supplier (stone + crates). Plan **≈ 25 t** | ≈ 485 m² per 20' at 25 t, if MINYALA weighs 50 kg/m² (estimate; a similar Egyptian limestone weighs 53.5 kg/m², giving ≈ 454 m²). ZIM may charge an overweight fee above 25 t on some lanes. Road limit in France: 44 t total for the truck. |
| Commodity | "Natural limestone tiles, 30×60×2 cm, in wooden crates, non-hazardous" + **HS 6802.92** (honed/calibrated) or **6802.29** (simply sawn) | Bookings may be refused without a 6-digit HS code. Ask the supplier which finish it is. |
| Incoterm / payment | **FOB Damietta** if you book the freight; prepaid | If the supplier sells CFR/CPT Le Havre, they book the freight and you don't need this quote. |
| Dates | Cargo ready date, number of containers, how often | |

## Customs and documents (import into France)

- **Duty: 0 %** for Egyptian-origin tiles, otherwise 1.7 % (codes 6802 92 00 / 6802 29 00) ([TARIC, 13/10/2026](https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/dds2/taric/measures.jsp?Lang=en&Taric=6802920000&Country=EG&SimDate=20261013)). The 0 % needs **proof of origin**: a **EUR.1** with "TRANSITIONAL RULES" in box 7, or an origin declaration on the invoice (by any exporter only up to 6,000 €; above that only an approved exporter) ([French customs](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-12/30/PJ4%20PEM_Synth%C3%A8se%20des%20preuves_MAJ122025.pdf), [Spanish customs, 27/04/2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/en_gb/aduanas/novedades/2026/abril/27/aplicacion-convenio-pem-sobre-normas-egipto.html)).
- **EORI number** (free, needed to import): request it on douane.gouv.fr (SOPRANO EORI); the format is FR + SIREN, i.e. FR985289065 ([douane.gouv.fr](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/demarche/enregistrer-votre-entreprise-aupres-de-la-douane-numero-eori)).
- **Import VAT 20 %**: paid by autoliquidation on the VAT return if the business has a French VAT number. Businesses under the VAT exemption (franchise en base) or the simplified regime must contact their tax office (SIE) first ([douane.gouv.fr](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/demarche/beneficier-automatiquement-de-lautoliquidation-de-la-tva-limport)).
- **Customs broker**: optional, but most small importers use a registered customs representative (RDE) or the freight forwarder ([douane.gouv.fr](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/fiche/le-representant-en-douane-enregistre)).
- **From the supplier**: commercial invoice, packing list (weights, crates, m²), bill of lading, EUR.1, and the **Declaration of Performance / CE marking** for natural stone floor tiles (EN 12058 / EN 12057; EN 1341 for outdoor paving) before selling the tiles in France. Wooden crates must carry the ISPM 15 treatment mark (not checked here).
- **Egyptian side**: the supplier must be on Egypt's exporters' register; Egypt's 2026 export fee targets raw blocks, not finished tiles.

## Good to know

- ZIM is being acquired by Hapag-Lloyd (agreed 16/02/2026, closing targeted late 2026). Ask how long the quote stays valid.
- Get two or three quotes: ZIM, another line (via a forwarder), and a forwarder door-to-door price Damietta → Gennevilliers, including Le Havre handling (THC), free days, haulage or barge, and customs.
- If the quote is for an **export buyer** instead: origin Le Havre, the buyer as consignee, and use CPT/CIP rather than CFR/CIF for containers.
